What a proper voice-agent project includes

  1. Map the calls. List customer questions, supported languages, booking rules, countries, business hours and escalation paths.
  2. Confirm privacy requirements. Document caller notice, consent, recordings/transcripts, retention and sensitive-data restrictions.
  3. Build a sandbox flow. Test synthetic and approved real-world examples, including silence, accents, interruptions and failed lookups.
  4. Connect the provider. Configure an authorized Twilio or equivalent test number, signed HTTPS webhooks and environment-specific credentials.
  5. Integrate calendars and CRMs. Use platform APIs and real free/busy checks; log external changes so double bookings and lost leads can be detected.
  6. Supervise launch. Agree acceptance metrics for calls completed, correct booking, handoffs and safe recovery from provider outages.

Frequently asked questions

Can a voice agent book directly into Google Calendar?

Yes, that can be part of a custom implementation when the customer supplies authorized OAuth access, approved booking rules and a test calendar. The public demo uses a local database and ICS export, not a live Google Calendar connection.

Will the agent replace every receptionist?

No. It can automate carefully scoped routine conversations. Staff escalation, emergency messages and unusual requests still require explicit business rules.

Does it work in Urdu, Hindi or other languages?

Language and accent support depend on the selected speech-to-text and text-to-speech provider. We scope and test each required language instead of assuming perfect recognition.

How much does AI voice automation cost?

Cost depends on phone provider fees, call volume, languages, AI usage, booking/CRM integration complexity and ongoing monitoring. Share your call workflow for a scope-based estimate.

Ready to scope your workflow?

Useful details: business location/time zone, current phone provider, monthly call volume, example calls, calendar/CRM platform, escalation rules and preferred languages.
