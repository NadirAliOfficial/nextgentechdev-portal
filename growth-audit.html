Frequently asked questions

Does the audit prove my website is indexed or ranking on Google?

No. The checks report HTML indexability hints only. Verified indexed pages, impressions and search queries require your own Google Search Console data.

Does this measure mobile speed or Core Web Vitals?

No. The tool checks HTML signals such as viewport configuration. It does not run Lighthouse, collect Chrome user field data or report real loading speed. Use Google PageSpeed Insights for those metrics.

Will the scanner see private pages or log in to my website?

No. Only public HTTP(S) websites can be scanned. Private/internal IPs, local networks, unusual ports and credentials are blocked. A site's robots instructions may prevent a scan.

Is the scan anonymous?

Your public website URL is sent to our scanner, which visits that URL from its server. The server may record ordinary access metadata; no website credentials are requested. Report downloads are generated in your browser.

Does a perfect checklist score mean great SEO or lots of sales?

No. It is a limited, weighted technical checklist. Real rankings, usability, conversions and performance require deeper measurements and human review.
