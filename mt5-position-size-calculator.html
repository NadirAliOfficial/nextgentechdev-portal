Why MT5 lot sizing is not identical for every symbol

Forex pairs, gold, indices, crypto CFDs and synthetic symbols can use different contract sizes, tick sizes, tick values and account currencies. A generic calculator cannot safely assume those values. In MT5, check the symbol specification for tick size, tick value, contract size, minimum volume and volume step before using a live order.

XAUUSD position sizing

Gold often behaves differently from major forex pairs because brokers can quote different digits and contract specifications. Rather than hard-coding a universal XAUUSD pip value, use your broker's symbol data. For automated systems, the EA should read symbol properties directly and normalize the requested volume to the broker's minimum, maximum and step constraints.

Risk controls a production EA should enforce

  • Maximum percentage risk per trade and across all open positions.
  • Broker volume minimum, maximum and step normalization.
  • Stop-loss distance validation before sending the order.
  • Spread and slippage limits where the strategy requires them.
  • Daily loss or equity drawdown protection when specified.
  • Clear logging when a trade is rejected or resized.
