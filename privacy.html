Privacy and project inquiry data

This page explains the information submitted through the website and how it is used for project communication.

Information you may provide

Project inquiry forms and live chat may collect your name, organization, email or contact handle, project requirements, budget range, and files you choose to attach.

Why it is used

Submitted information is used to review project requirements, respond to inquiries, prepare estimates or proposals, communicate about delivery, and protect the website from misuse.

Do not send secrets unnecessarily

Please avoid submitting passwords, private API keys, seed phrases, full payment-card data, government identifiers, or production credentials through public forms or chat. Sensitive access should be exchanged only through an agreed secure method when required for a project.

Third-party services

The website may rely on hosting, messaging, analytics, payment, escrow, email, or infrastructure providers where enabled. Their own terms and privacy practices can apply to data processed through those services.

Retention and deletion requests

Project communication may be retained as needed for business records, delivery, security, and dispute handling. For a request concerning information you submitted, contact NextGen Tech Solutions through the website's listed WhatsApp or Telegram channels.

Security

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are used, but no internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.
