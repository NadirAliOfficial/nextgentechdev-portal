How to use these resources

  1. Define the exact problem. Start from the workflow, strategy rules, risk limits or business process you want to automate.
  2. Use the relevant calculator or guide. Check assumptions and identify missing inputs before development begins.
  3. Turn the result into acceptance criteria. Specify what must happen, what must never happen, and how failures should be handled.
  4. Test before production. Use demo accounts, staging systems, logs and repeatable test cases before relying on automation.
