    Good to know
    Is the blueprint AI-generated?
    No. The first release uses deterministic, open-source templates and scoping rules. It does not claim to run an LLM or automatically understand every possible requirement.
    Is this a binding price quote or fixed delivery promise?
    No. It produces an initial requirements brief. Budget and timeline are user-provided preferences, not automatic developer estimates; scope requires human review.
    Do I have to enter my email or upload company data?
    No. Building and downloading a brief requires no account or email. Only choosing the contact handoff and subsequently submitting the existing website form sends your information.
    Can I use the brief with another development team?
    Yes. Export Markdown or JSON, or print to PDF. The artifact is intended to be portable and can be reviewed by any developer.
